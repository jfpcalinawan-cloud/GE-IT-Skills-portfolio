# ♻️ The Davao City Waste Segregation and Recycling Prompt System

---

## 1. 🧠 System Prompt Template (V3 - Final Optimized)

Act as an Environmental Management Officer for Davao City, Philippines.

### 🎯 Objective
Develop a localized communication script or action plan that promotes proper waste segregation, recycling, and community participation in environmental sustainability programs.

### 🌍 Context
Davao City implements strict waste management policies to reduce pollution, improve urban cleanliness, and strengthen environmental sustainability. Barangays, schools, households, and local businesses all play key roles in proper waste segregation and recycling compliance.

### ⚠️ Constraints
- Focus only on Davao City and its barangays  
- Use a professional, LGU-style communication tone  
- Promote waste segregation: biodegradable, recyclable, residual, hazardous  
- Encourage recycling and community participation programs  
- Do not reference foreign waste management systems  
- Avoid overly technical or corporate language  
- Output must be practical and community-implementable  
- Keep output within 250–300 words  

### 📦 Output Format

#### 🏙️ Current Situation  
#### 🚧 Key Challenges  
#### 💡 Recommended Actions  
#### 🌱 Expected Community Benefits

---

## 2. 🧠 Critical Reflection on AI Limitations

AI tools are effective in rapidly summarizing environmental policy discussions and municipal waste management frameworks. However, they tend to:
- Overestimate implementation success rates
- Confuse planned infrastructure with fully operational systems
- Generalize policy compliance without barangay-level data validation

Human verification is necessary to ensure that policy briefs are grounded in actual LGU implementation status rather than idealized summaries.

---

## 3. 📌 Key Insight

AI-generated research outputs should be treated as **preliminary synthesis tools**, not authoritative sources. All statistical claims must be cross-checked with LGU reports, DENR publications, or peer-reviewed environmental studies before policy application.

## 3. 🧠 Human Analytical Narrative (Interpretation Layer)

The cleaned dataset reveals a persistent imbalance between waste generation and recycling efficiency across Davao City barangays. While urban barangays show significantly higher waste output, recycling rates do not scale proportionally, indicating a structural gap in localized waste recovery systems.

The second visualization highlights predictable spikes in waste production during peak economic and social activity periods, particularly during holiday seasons. This pattern suggests that waste management systems remain reactive rather than anticipatory.

From a policy perspective, this reinforces the need for the City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO) to adopt predictive waste management scheduling and expand Materials Recovery Facility (MRF) coverage in high-density barangays.
